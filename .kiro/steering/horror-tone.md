# Horror tone and visual rules

- Near-black world. Light comes only from the lantern and ghost eyes.
- Dread over gore. No blood or graphic content. Fear comes from silence, darkness and things that moved while you weren't looking.
- Ghouls never move on screen while the player's eyes are open. Their position simply changes.
- UI text is short, lowercase, whispered in tone ("it moved.", "don't blink.").
- Sound matters as much as visuals: whispers, distant bells, wind, a heartbeat that speeds up with the fear meter.
- Colors: background #050507, fog #0d0f14, lantern #e8a33d, ghost eyes #c8f0ff, danger #8a1c1c.
