# Product: Don't Blink

A browser horror game set in a dark, foggy graveyard, played with the eyes and voice.

- Ghouls advance only when the player blinks (webcam).
- Closing the eyes for 2+ seconds triggers "grave sight": hidden things appear, but ghouls may move.
- The player banishes ghouls by speaking spells. Shouting attracts ghouls.
- Final boss: a staring contest. The player must not blink for 10–15 seconds.
- Every action has a keyboard fallback.
- All vision processing runs on the player's device. No video is uploaded.

Built for the Kiroween 2026 hackathon. Judges score potential value, Kiro usage, and quality/design.
